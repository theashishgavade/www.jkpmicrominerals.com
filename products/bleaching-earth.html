Product Overview

Bleaching Earth

Bleaching Earth is a specially processed adsorbent material designed to remove pigments, impurities, trace metals and other undesirable substances from oils and fats during the refining process. It is primarily produced from selected clay minerals and processed to achieve high adsorption capacity and consistent refining performance.

With its high surface area, controlled adsorption properties and effective decolorization performance, Bleaching Earth plays an important role in improving the colour, clarity and overall quality of refined oils and fats.
