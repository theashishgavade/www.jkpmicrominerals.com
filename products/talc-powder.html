Product Overview

Talc Powder

Talc Powder is a naturally occurring hydrated magnesium silicate known for its softness, chemical inertness, excellent whiteness and lubricating properties.

It is widely used as a functional filler in plastics, paints, paper, rubber, ceramics, cosmetics and industrial formulations to improve processing, dimensional stability and product performance.
