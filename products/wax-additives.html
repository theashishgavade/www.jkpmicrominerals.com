Product Overview

Wax Additives

Wax Additives are specialized performance additives designed to modify and enhance the surface properties of coatings, inks, polishes and other industrial formulations. They can provide improved slip, scratch resistance, abrasion resistance, rub resistance, water repellency and surface feel.

Due to their surface-modifying and protective properties, Wax Additives play an important role in improving durability, appearance, handling and overall surface performance across a wide range of industrial applications.
