Product Overview

Silica Powder & Silica Sand

Silica is one of the most widely used industrial minerals due to its excellent hardness, chemical resistance and thermal stability. It is available in powder and sand forms to suit various industrial processing requirements.

Silica Powder is commonly used as a functional filler and performance enhancer, while Silica Sand is widely utilized in foundry, construction, glass and filtration applications.
