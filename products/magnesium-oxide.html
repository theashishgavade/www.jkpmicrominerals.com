Product Overview

Magnesium Oxide

Magnesium Oxide (MgO) is a versatile inorganic compound widely used as an activator, neutralizing agent and performance additive across numerous industrial sectors.

Due to its high purity, excellent thermal resistance and controlled reactivity, Magnesium Oxide is extensively used in rubber compounds, refractories, fertilizers, animal feed, wastewater treatment and specialty applications.
