Product Overview

Mica Powder

Mica Powder is a naturally occurring silicate mineral known for its plate-like structure, excellent insulation properties and chemical stability. It is widely used as a functional filler across numerous industrial sectors.

Due to its unique lamellar structure, Mica improves dimensional stability, weather resistance, anti-cracking performance and surface finish in various formulations.
