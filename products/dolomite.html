Product Overview

Dolomite Powder

Dolomite is a naturally occurring mineral composed of Calcium Magnesium Carbonate. It is widely used as a functional filler and extender in numerous industrial applications due to its excellent whiteness, hardness and chemical stability.

Dolomite Powder improves product performance, reduces formulation costs and enhances physical properties across plastics, paints, rubber, ceramics and construction products.
