Product Overview

Zinc Oxide

Zinc Oxide (ZnO) is a versatile inorganic compound known for its excellent activating, reinforcing, UV-resistant and protective properties. It is widely used in rubber manufacturing, ceramics, paints, coatings and specialty chemical applications.

Due to its high purity, chemical stability and multifunctional performance, Zinc Oxide plays an important role in enhancing product quality and manufacturing efficiency.
