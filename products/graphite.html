Product Overview

Graphite Powder

Graphite is a naturally occurring crystalline form of carbon known for its outstanding conductivity, lubricating properties and high-temperature resistance.

It is widely used in foundry coatings, brake linings, batteries, lubricants, refractories, conductive compounds and various industrial applications requiring thermal and electrical performance.
