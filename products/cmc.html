Product Overview

CMC

Carboxymethyl Cellulose (CMC) is a water-soluble cellulose derivative widely used as a thickener, stabilizer, binder, water-retention agent and rheology modifier. It provides effective viscosity control, suspension stability and formulation consistency across a wide range of water-based industrial applications.

Due to its excellent thickening, water-retention and film-forming properties, CMC plays an important role in improving workability, stability and application performance in paints, coatings, adhesives, construction materials and other specialty formulations.
