Product Overview

Aluminium Trihydrate

Aluminium Trihydrate (ATH) is a versatile inorganic mineral filler widely used as a flame retardant, smoke suppressant and functional filler in various industrial applications. It offers excellent thermal stability, low toxicity and good compatibility with a wide range of polymer and industrial formulations.

When exposed to elevated temperatures, ATH releases chemically bound water, helping reduce heat release, suppress smoke and contribute to improved fire resistance. Its controlled particle size, purity and consistent quality make it suitable for demanding applications across electrical, construction, ceramic and specialty industries.
