Product Overview

Perlite

Perlite is a naturally occurring volcanic glass that expands when heated, producing a lightweight, porous material with excellent insulation and absorption properties.

Due to its low density, thermal resistance and moisture management capabilities, Perlite is widely used in construction materials, horticulture, filtration systems and specialty industrial products.
