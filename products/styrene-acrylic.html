Product Overview

Styrene Acrylic

Styrene Acrylic is a versatile synthetic polymer dispersion produced from styrene and acrylic monomers. It is widely used as a binder in water-based paints, coatings, adhesives and construction formulations, offering good adhesion, durability, water resistance and weathering performance.

Due to its balanced film-forming properties, adhesion and resistance characteristics, Styrene Acrylic plays an important role in improving coating performance, surface protection and formulation stability across a wide range of industrial applications.
