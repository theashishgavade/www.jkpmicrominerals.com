Product Overview

Synthetic Barytes

Synthetic Barytes is a chemically processed form of Barium Sulphate designed to provide superior purity, brightness and particle size consistency compared to naturally occurring barytes.

It is widely used in premium paints, coatings, plastics, inks, powder coatings and specialty applications where high whiteness and controlled performance are critical.
