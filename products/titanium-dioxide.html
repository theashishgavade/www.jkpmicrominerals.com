Titanium Dioxide (TiO₂) Replacement Extender

Cost-effective specialty extender designed to partially replace Titanium Dioxide while maintaining opacity, brightness and overall coating performance in paints, coatings and industrial formulations.

Product Overview

TiO₂ Replacement Extender

Titanium Dioxide is one of the most expensive raw materials used in paint and coating formulations. Our TiO₂ Replacement Extender helps manufacturers optimize formulation costs without significantly affecting opacity, whiteness and performance.

It is specifically designed for decorative paints, industrial coatings, primers, putties and other applications where cost optimization and performance balance are important.
