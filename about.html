About JKP Microminerals

Building Trust Through Quality, Innovation & Reliable Mineral Solutions Since 2002

Established in 2002, JKP Microminerals (I) Pvt. Ltd. has grown into one of India's trusted manufacturers, processors and exporters of premium micro-minerals, specialty fillers and industrial additives. With decades of industry expertise, we proudly serve customers across diverse industrial sectors by delivering products that meet the highest standards of quality, consistency and performance.

Our integrated approach—from responsible sourcing and mineral processing to quality assurance, packaging and timely delivery—enables us to provide dependable solutions tailored to the unique requirements of every customer. Backed by modern manufacturing facilities, experienced professionals and a strong distribution network, we continue to build long-term partnerships based on trust, reliability and technical excellence in both domestic and international markets.
