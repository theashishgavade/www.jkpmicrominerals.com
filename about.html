Company Overview

Trusted Partner For Industrial Minerals & Specialty Chemical Solutions

JKP Microminerals (I) Pvt. Ltd. has established itself as a trusted manufacturer, processor, supplier and distributor of industrial minerals, specialty fillers, additives and performance chemicals serving multiple industries across India.
